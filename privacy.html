Who we are

The Social Media Command Centre is an internal tool used by Volta ([Volta's legal name and address]) to manage messages and comments sent to Volta's own Facebook Page, Instagram account and LinkedIn Page. Only invited Volta staff can sign in to it.

What information we collect

  • Comments, replies, mentions, tags and direct messages that people send to or about Volta's own social media accounts.
  • The public name, username and profile link of the person who sent them, as provided by the platform.
  • The date and time of each interaction, and which post or conversation it belongs to.
  • For Volta staff: work email address, and a record of actions taken in the tool (for example, who approved a reply and when).

We only receive information that the platforms make available to Volta as the owner of these accounts. We do not collect information from people's private profiles, and we do not buy or sell personal information.

How we use it

  • To show Volta staff new interactions in one place so they can respond promptly.
  • To sort interactions by priority and flag comments that may be abusive, spam, or contain personal information, so a person can review them.
  • To suggest draft replies. Every reply is reviewed, edited if needed, and approved by a Volta staff member before it is sent. Nothing is posted, hidden, deleted or blocked automatically.
  • To prepare a daily summary for Volta staff.

Automated and AI processing

[Once enabled:] The text of interactions may be processed by an AI service provider ([Anthropic]) to sort them and suggest draft replies. The provider processes this text on Volta's behalf and [does not use it to train its models]. A person always makes the final decision.

Where it is stored and who can see it

Information is stored in a secure database hosted by Supabase ([United States region]) and the website is hosted by Vercel. Access is limited to invited Volta staff who sign in with a Volta email address. Platform access tokens are stored encrypted and are never shown in the website.

How long we keep it

We keep interaction data for [90 days], then delete it, unless it is needed longer for an open issue. Staff activity records are kept for [12 months].

Your choices and deletion requests

You can delete your own comment or message on the platform at any time. To ask Volta to delete information about you from this tool, email [privacy contact email, for example privacy@voltaeffect.com] with the platform and your username. We will confirm deletion within [30 days].

Volta can disconnect its accounts from this tool at any time, which stops all new data collection.
