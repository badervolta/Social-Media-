Settings

Connect your social accounts

Click a button, sign in to the platform, and choose the Volta page. Your social media passwords are never shared with this dashboard, and nothing is ever posted without a person's approval.

Before you connect

  • Facebook: you need full control of the Volta Facebook Page.
  • Instagram: Volta's Instagram must be a business account linked to that Facebook Page. Connecting Facebook connects it too.
  • LinkedIn: you need to be an admin of the Volta LinkedIn Page.
  • You can disconnect any account at any time.

Assumptions

  • One company account per platform: a LinkedIn page, a Facebook page and an Instagram business profile.
  • Any invited team member can review and approve. Admins manage connected accounts.
  • Volta's time zone is Atlantic (AST). The digest covers the 24 hours before the chosen digest time (1:00 p.m. by default). Admins can change the time on the digest page.
  • Urgent means media requests, billing problems, stated deadlines and possible inappropriate content.
  • The voice is warm, short and plain, with Canadian spelling and a personal sign-off.

Safety rules built in

  • No social accounts connected yet. Data is stored in Supabase and only signed-in, invited Volta staff can read it.
  • The browser can never delete real data, edit message content, or send anything.
  • No reply can be approved while it still contains a [placeholder].
  • Offensive comments are blurred until you choose to read them.
  • Every action is written to the item's activity history.
